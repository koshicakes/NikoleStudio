Privacy Policy

How Nikole Studio handles your information.

This page explains what we collect for inquiries and bookings, how we use it, and how you can request updates or deletion.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number, and Messenger handle.
  • Booking details such as selected service, package, preferred date, preferred time, backdrop, and session notes.
  • Reference materials or mood boards you voluntarily send for session planning.

How We Use Your Information

  • To review, confirm, reschedule, or follow up on booking requests.
  • To prepare the studio setup, props, backdrops, and session flow.
  • To answer questions sent through the contact form, email, or Messenger.
  • To maintain booking records and avoid schedule conflicts.

Photos And Creative References

  • Client photos are used for editing, delivery, and gallery preparation connected to the session.
  • Portfolio or marketing use should only happen with client consent or an agreed arrangement.
  • References you send are used only to understand the creative direction for your booking.

Storage And Sharing

  • Booking details are kept only as long as needed for studio operations, records, and client communication.
  • Nikole Studio does not sell personal information.
  • Information may be shared only with trusted studio staff or service providers when needed to complete your session.

Your Requests

  • You may ask Nikole Studio to correct, update, or delete your personal information.
  • You may withdraw consent for optional portfolio or marketing use.
  • For privacy concerns, contact the studio through Messenger or email at hello@nikolestudio.ph.

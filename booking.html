Booking Policy

Please review these notes before sending your request so the studio can reserve, prepare, and confirm your session smoothly.

  • Your slot is not final until Nikole Studio reviews your request and sends a confirmation.
  • Fully booked dates are unavailable in the calendar, while partially booked dates show only remaining time slots.
  • Please arrive on time. Late arrival may shorten the session depending on the next scheduled booking.
  • Rescheduling requests should be made at least 48 hours before the appointment.
  • Backdrop, prop, outfit, HMUA, and event coverage notes should be included in the request when relevant.
  • Personal information is handled according to the Nikole Studio Privacy Policy.
